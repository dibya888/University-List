Methodology & caveats

Every program was checked individually against its official university or DAAD page. Fields the research could not confirm from an official source are marked not officially confirmed rather than guessed — filter or search freely, but re-verify anything italicised before you rely on it.

Deadlines fall into three columns in the full dataset: a reference cycle (the most recently confirmed date, often 2026), a Summer 2027 projection, and the Winter 2027 target shown here — projected forward one year from a confirmed annual pattern where one existed. Treat projected dates as planning estimates, not final answers (each Winter 2027 deadline now starts with a status tag — CONFIRMED, REFERENCE VERIFIED, ESTIMATED or CONFLICTING — and Backup-tier programs appear in the Explorer only); re-check the official page two to three months before you actually apply.
